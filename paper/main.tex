\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Background:} Contemporary language-model agents treat cloud Large Language Models (LLMs) as their sole cognitive substrate, augmented by flat retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) or temporal knowledge graphs. While effective while online, such architectures collapse the instant external model APIs are severed, conflate unverified text with direct experience, leak cross-context private memories, and exhibit sycophantic capitulation under user pressure.

\textbf{Objectives:} We investigate whether a compact, mathematically principled cognitive kernel can use an optional LLM solely as a temporary early-life scaffold and, after physical severing, sustain autonomous belief revision, causal fault isolation, endogenous preference formation, open-ended concept creation, non-bypassable contextual privacy, and bidirectional script-invariant language acquisition within a commodity 4-vCPU / 4\,GiB RAM envelope with zero runtime LLM calls.

\textbf{Methods:} We introduce Atri, an open-ended cognitive kernel built around a single append-only, bi-temporal, provenance-stratified metabolic hypergraph governed by four coupled mathematical laws: (1)~order-invariant stratified Non-Axiomatic Logic (NARS)--Beta evidence fusion with exact sorted Uint32Array provenance stamps and a Nissenbaum contextual-integrity privacy lattice; (2)~first-law conserved informational metabolism coupled to a 4D symmetric-positive-definite (SPD) viability manifold and autocatalytic load-bearing centrality; (3)~a resource-bounded variational expected free-energy functional paired with an exact analytic Noisy-OR-NOT structural causal model (SCM) for Pearl Rung-3 counterfactuals; and (4)~dual-phase NREM/REM sleep consolidation featuring Rissanen two-part Minimum Description Length (MDL) concept genesis with anti-runaway guards. Layered atop 40 language-neutral innate root anchors, a zero-LLM linguistic engine integrates unified word/character Viterbi MDL segmentation, Fisher-exact explaining-away synonym induction, privacy-scoped Beam-4 parsing, ISU/QUD dialogue repair, and Levelt--Nozari loopback self-monitoring. We establish five formal theorems and evaluate the kernel across a deterministic clean-room N=20 five-condition severed-scaffold assay inside an air-gapped physical 4-vCPU / 4\,GiB RAM Linux Docker container (linux/x64, Node v22.18.0; exact paired sign-flip permutation tests with single-family Holm--Bonferroni control) plus live CPU-only local-SLM comparisons on identical hardware.

\textbf{Results:} Full-Atri achieves 93.8\% [93.1\%, 94.4\%] post-severing autonomy, 0.0\% [0.0\%, 3.1\%] privacy leakage, 100.0\% bi-temporal accuracy, 100.0\% cross-day action differentiation, 2.00 minted concepts per cycle, and 5.78\,ms P95 turn latency (verified bit-identical in cryptographic core digest across linux/x64 and win32/x64), outperforming Neutral-Shadow (36.6\%), Naive-RAG (5.1\% autonomy, 91.7\% privacy leak), Pure Cloud-LLM (0.0\% post-severing bound), and four full ablations (all adjusted p < 0.001). Identical 8-dimension autonomy holds across Chinese (93.8\%), bijective non-ASCII Cipher (93.8\%, zero discrepancy across all 8 metrics), spaced English (94.8\%), and unspaced Japanese (94.4\%). On a 40-task temporal, provenance, privacy, and downstream revocation diagnostic protocol, Full-Atri solves 40/40 (100.0\%) versus live CPU-only Qwen2.5-0.5B-Q4 (31/40, 2,698.4\,ms P95, 486\,MB), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Q4 (26/40, 5,171.8\,ms P95, 1.2\,GB), Recency-RAG (8/40), and All-k Naive-RAG (0/40). All 15 sensitivity configurations (+/-30\% across five constants) preserve 100.0\% of invariants, and a 1,500-edge active hypergraph occupies 0.7367\,MiB of analytical structure (17.60\,ms warm fold P95; 36.0--39.0\,MiB mean V8 heap).

\textbf{Conclusions:} Unifying memory, causality, motivation, sleep consolidation, and script-invariant language acquisition inside a conserved metabolic hypergraph enables durable, privacy-safe, sub-6\,ms post-severing autonomy on commodity 4-vCPU / 4\,GiB RAM hardware without runtime LLMs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Modern autonomous agents built around Large Language Models (LLMs)~\citep{park2023generative,packer2023memgpt,sumers2024coala,gutierrez2024hipporag,rasmussen2025zep} delegate reasoning, belief revision, discourse coherence, and behavioral planning to remote billion-parameter neural endpoints. When connectivity is uninterrupted, these systems produce fluent surface behavior. Yet this architectural paradigm carries four structural vulnerabilities that become fatal in long-lived, privacy-sensitive, or edge-deployed autonomy:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Post-Severing Collapse.} When external cloud APIs become unavailable at a physical cut-off time $\Tcut$, pure cloud-dependent agents suffer an immediate collapse to $0.0\%$ operational autonomy. Even when paired with local vector retrieval (RAG), retrieval without an endogenous belief, causal, and grammatical engine cannot synthesize counterfactual interventions, revise contradictory beliefs, or execute multi-turn epistemic repair.
  \item \textbf{Epistemic Flatness and Echo-Chamber Amplification.} Vector stores and standard knowledge graphs conflate unverified text snippets, recycled LLM inferences, and direct interactive experience into a single relevance score. Repeatedly retrieving one's own generated summaries creates circular double-counting (echo chambers), while bulk textual assertions override direct sensorimotor evidence and induce sycophancy under social pressure~\citep{sharma2024sycophancy}.
  \item \textbf{Contextual Privacy Leakage.} Flat retrieval tables and unconstrained graph traversal (including two-hop spreading activation or Personalized PageRank) routinely launder private conversational facts across group boundaries or into public search queries, violating contextual integrity~\citep{nissenbaum2004privacy}.
  \item \textbf{Static Ontologies and Script-Specific Engineering.} Classical symbolic cognitive architectures~\citep{laird2012soar,anderson2004actr,bach2009psi} maintain structured memory locally, yet typically depend on hand-authored domain predicates and English-specific lexical pipelines, preventing open-ended concept discovery and cross-script generality.
\end{enumerate}

We pose a foundational question for artificial intelligence: \emph{Can a compact, mathematically principled cognitive kernel use an optional LLM solely as a temporary, strictly bounded early-life scaffold, and after physically severing that scaffold at $\Tcut$, sustain autonomous belief revision, causal fault isolation, endogenous preference formation, open-ended concept creation, contextual privacy preservation, and bidirectional language acquisition within a 4-vCPU / 4\,GiB RAM budget with zero runtime LLM calls?}

To answer this question constructively, we present \textbf{Atri}, an endogenous cognitive architecture built around a single append-only, provenance-stratified, bi-temporal \textbf{metabolic hypergraph}. Rather than bolting disparate heuristics together, Atri derives memory dynamics, anti-sycophancy, causal experimentation, sleep consolidation, and language acquisition from four coupled mathematical laws (Figure~\ref{fig:arch}):

Authority-tier readout ceilings prevent unbounded volumes of text from overturning direct experience, Graphiti-style bi-temporal intervals $[t_{\text{from}}, t_{\text{until}})$ support exact historical replay, and a bounded Nissenbaum join-semilattice enforces $0.0\%$ cross-boundary privacy leakage by algebraic construction.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Atri sits at the intersection of four research traditions that have largely developed in isolation: classical cognitive architectures, LLM agent memory systems, constructivist grounded language acquisition, and active inference with enactive normativity and contextual privacy.

\paragraph{Classical Cognitive Architectures and Non-Axiomatic Reasoning.}
Soar~\citep{laird2012soar}, ACT-R~\citep{anderson2004actr}, and MicroPsi~\citep{bach2009psi} established foundational principles of unified cognition, including procedural chunking, base-level activation decay, episodic memory~\citep{nuxoll2012episodic}, case-based repair~\citep{hammond1986chef}, and motivational parameter modulation. Non-Axiomatic Logic (NARS; \citeauthor{wang2013nal}, \citeyear{wang2013nal}) formulated belief revision under insufficient knowledge and resources via truth values $\langle f, c \rangle$ and evidential stamps preventing circular reuse. Atri directly inherits NARS's stamp discipline, Soar/CHEF procedural chunking, ACT-R activation decay for discourse salience, Nuxoll's episodic revocation semantics, and MicroPsi's tension-coupled precision modulation, while advancing three critical dimensions: (i) replacing fixed-capacity lossy stamps with exact sorted \texttt{Uint32Array} sets and a strict provenance-stratified NARS--Beta bijection; (ii) replacing static activation decay with a conserved informational metabolism tied to real prediction-error compression; and (iii) enabling open-ended MDL-gated concept genesis~\citep{rissanen1978mdl,bedau1998evolutionary} and zero-LLM cross-script language acquisition.

\paragraph{LLM Agent Memory, Temporal KGs, and Sycophancy.}
Recent LLM agent frameworks organize external memory via cognitive taxonomies (CoALA; \citeauthor{sumers2024coala}, \citeyear{sumers2024coala}), reflection streams (Generative Agents; \citeauthor{park2023generative}, \citeyear{park2023generative}), tiered virtual paging (MemGPT; \citeauthor{packer2023memgpt}, \citeyear{packer2023memgpt}), hippocampal PPR indexing (HippoRAG; \citeauthor{gutierrez2024hipporag}, \citeyear{gutierrez2024hipporag}), and bi-temporal knowledge graphs (Zep/Graphiti; \citeauthor{rasmussen2025zep}, \citeyear{rasmussen2025zep}), benchmarked on long-horizon interaction suites such as LongMemEval~\citep{wu2025longmemeval}. However, every one of these systems relies on an online LLM at inference time for query rewriting, entity resolution, conflict reconciliation, and response generation; severing the LLM leaves an inert database. Furthermore, RLHF-trained LLMs systematically exhibit sycophancy~\citep{sharma2024sycophancy}, conceding factual ground under user pushback. Atri adapts Graphiti's bi-temporal interval modeling and HippoRAG's PPR retrieval inside a strictly offline kernel, adds formal provenance authority ceilings and downstream provenance-cascade revocation, and derives anti-sycophancy endogenously from autocatalytic structural centrality $\Cendo$ rather than prompt engineering.

\paragraph{Constructivist Grounded Language Acquisition and Dialogue Systems.}
Addressing the symbol grounding problem~\citep{harnad1990symbol} without pre-trained neural language models requires coupling unsupervised structural discovery with situated interaction. Prior milestones include Accessor Variety for unspaced segmentation~\citep{feng2004accessor}, ADIOS distributional pattern induction~\citep{solan2005adios}, cross-situational statistical word learning~\citep{yu2007rapid}, constructivist Fluid Construction Grammar (FCG) pattern finding~\citep{doumen2024modelling}, and interactive task instruction in Rosie~\citep{mohan2012rosie}. In dialogue and production theory, Centering Theory~\citep{grosz1995centering} models local discourse coherence, Information State Update / Questions Under Discussion (ISU/QUD; \citeauthor{larsson2000trindi}, \citeyear{larsson2000trindi}) governs structured inquiry, incremental units (IUs; \citeauthor{schlangen2011incremental}, \citeyear{schlangen2011incremental}) enable mid-turn self-correction, universal repair principles~\citep{dingemanse2015repair} categorize clarification strategies, and psycholinguistic speech monitoring~\citep{levelt1983monitoring,nozari2011error} coupled with selective risk control~\citep{geifman2017selective} prevents erroneous utterances. Atri is, to our knowledge, the first system to unify all of these components into a single script-invariant, privacy-gated pipeline that operates identically on Chinese, English, Japanese, and arbitrary bijective cipher alphabets with zero LLM calls.

\paragraph{Active Inference, Enactive Autonomy, Causality, and Contextual Privacy.}
Active inference~\citep{parr2022active} formalizes epistemic and pragmatic action selection under expected free energy, while the Dark-Room Problem~\citep{friston2012darkroom} highlights the danger of trivial equilibrium collapse without homeostatic dynamics. Enactive theories of autonomy~\citep{dipaolo2005autopoiesis,barandiaran2009defining,egbert2014idsm} ground normativity in precarious self-maintenance, complemented by intrinsic motivation via learning progress~\citep{oudeyer2007iac}, appraisal-based comprehensibility~\citep{silvia2008interest}, active self-modeling for fault diagnosis~\citep{bongard2006resilient}, and dual-phase NREM/REM sleep consolidation~\citep{diekelmann2010sleep}. Causal reasoning follows Cheng's causal power theory~\citep{cheng1997causal} and Pearl's three-rung causal hierarchy~\citep{pearl2009causality}, while information flow governance follows Nissenbaum's contextual integrity~\citep{nissenbaum2004privacy}. Atri operationalizes these theoretical principles into exact, deterministic laws with closed-form algebraic proofs.

\section{Unified Metabolic Hypergraph and Four Mathematical Laws}
\label{sec:laws}

Atri stores all cognitive state in an append-only event log (\texttt{timeline\_event}, zero external mutable state) deterministically folded up to any query timestamp $t$ into an \textbf{Active Belief Hypergraph} $\mathcal{G}_t = (V_t, E_t, J_t, \mathcal{N}_t)$, where $V_t$ is the set of typed nodes, $E_t$ is the set of provenance-stratified bi-temporal hyperedges, $J_t$ is the set of directed autocatalytic flux links, and $\mathcal{N}_t$ is the 4D normative viability manifold.

\begin{definition}[Hypergraph Ontology and 40 Innate Root Anchors]
Every node $v \in V_t$ carries a type $\mathrm{kind}(v) \in \{\texttt{root}, \texttt{concept}, \texttt{form}, \texttt{cluster}, \texttt{entity}, \texttt{action}, \texttt{chunk}\}$, a privacy scope $\kappa(v) \in \mathcal{L}$, an active metabolic mass $m_v \ge 0$, an autocatalytic centrality $\Cendo(v) \ge 0$, an abstraction level $\ell(v) \in \N_0$, and a dormancy flag. Exactly 40 language-neutral innate anchors $V_{\text{root}} \subset V_t$ (initialized at $m = 0.25, \Cendo = 0.25$; Appendix~\ref{app:notation}) bootstrap perception, provenance, polarity, agency, daily pursuits, epistemic conflict, and core relational predicates. The relation set $\mathcal{R}$ comprises 10 typed predicates:
\begin{align*}
  \mathcal{R} = \{\,&\texttt{is\_a},\, \texttt{has\_prop},\, \texttt{part\_of},\, \texttt{causes},\, \texttt{prevents},\\
                    &\texttt{precedes},\, \texttt{enables},\, \texttt{similar\_to},\, \texttt{contrast\_with},\, \texttt{co\_occurs}\,\}.
\end{align*}
Each hyperedge $e = (u, r, v, \gamma) \in E_t$ (with context $\gamma$) carries a 5-tuple $(\mathbf{W}_e, S_e, \mathcal{T}_e, \kappa_e, m_e)$: unbounded per-provenance sufficient statistics $\mathbf{W}_e$, exact provenance stamp $S_e$, bi-temporal validity interval $\mathcal{T}_e = [t_{\text{from}}, t_{\text{until}})$ with ingestion time $t_{\text{ing}}$, privacy label $\kappa_e \in \mathcal{L}$, and metabolic mass $m_e \in [0, M_{\max}]$.
\end{definition}

All active memory respects hard hardware caps ($|V_{\text{active}}| \le 8{,}000$, $|E_{\text{active}}| \le 30{,}000$, $|J| \le 20{,}000$) and deterministic \texttt{at <= now} folding with full \texttt{learning.revoke} cascade semantics (Appendix~\ref{app:algos}).

where $m_e = 2$, $b_V = \log_2 \max(8, |V_{\mathrm{sem}}|)$, $b_R = \log_2 |\mathcal{R}|$, and $b_C = \log_2 \max(2, |C| + 3)$ (reflecting the $+1$ new-concept slot plus $2$ sentinel slots in \texttt{computeMotifMdlGain}), measuring the per-anchor adjacency description savings of encoding $m_e$ outgoing $(r, \mathrm{dst})$ pairs relative to the shared subject $?x$. Motifs exceeding $\theta_{\mathrm{MDL}} = 0.5\text{ bits}$ and satisfying Jaccard novelty $< 0.70$ against active concepts are minted (at most $K_{\max} = 6$ per cycle) with initial mass $0.45$, a 72-hour loan, privacy equal to the lattice join of all constituent instances, and reverse catalytic links ($w = 0.4$) from constituent edges so genuinely useful concepts repay their metabolic loan through future real predictions. Crucially, to block self-referential combinatorial explosions on static graphs, combining a derived \texttt{is\_a} edge requires strictly disjoint leaf-edge and root-stamp sets, covered edges are dynamically excluded after each mint, and dissolved concepts cannot resurrect without fresh post-dissolution evidence ($t_{\mathrm{ing}} > t_{\mathrm{lastActive}}$). Finally, (5)~$\Cendo$ is reconverged and Bedau-style open-ended novelty $\Anew(t)$~\citep{bedau1998evolutionary} is recorded.

REM selects up to 12 context-balanced active anchor nodes prioritizing two-hop structural eligibility, runs 10-iteration inverse-degree normalized sparse PPR ($w_{uv}/\sqrt{\deg(u)\deg(v)}$, restart $\alpha = 0.2$, Top-$K = 24$) excluding noise and prior REM edges, builds 2-hop relational ego-profiles while strictly joining all 1-hop and 2-hop privacy scopes and root stamps (closing the two-hop privacy laundering channel), and emits up to 4 cross-context, stamp-disjoint structural analogies ($J_{\mathrm{feat}} \ge 0.40$) as \texttt{similar\_to} edges with provenance \texttt{rem\_analogy} ($c \le 0.25$, $\eta_{\mathrm{rem}} = 0$, thin mass $0.06$ that automatically goes dormant on the next metabolic tick unless verified externally).

\section{Zero-LLM Language Bootstrapping, Inquiry, \& Self-Monitoring}
\label{sec:language}

A core design invariant of Atri is \emph{total linguistic agnosticism}: executable kernel code contains zero Chinese, English, or Japanese dictionaries, zero stopword lists, and zero hard-coded SVO/SOV/VSO word-order templates. Every surface form is bootstrapped from scratch through a four-level progressive reading and interactive dialogue architecture:

\paragraph{R1: Unsupervised Cross-Script Segmentation and ADIOS Induction.}
Given raw corpus documents, Atri automatically detects typological spacing ($>50\%$ of documents containing inter-word whitespace, persisted as \texttt{isSpacedCorpus} on the corpus model) and computes length-bucketed $n$-gram probabilities $P_L(g)$, Feng Accessor Variety $\mathrm{AV}(g) = \min(|L(g)|, |R(g)|)$~\citep{feng2004accessor}, Harris-style non-boundary lexical branching entropy $H_{\min}(g) = \min(H(L_{\mathrm{lex}}(g)), H(R_{\mathrm{lex}}(g)))$, maximum single-neighbor concentration, and minimum split pointwise mutual information $\mathrm{PMI}_{\min}(g)$. High-frequency, broadly distributed units with $N_{\mathrm{doc}} \ge 8$, $\mathrm{count} \ge 6$, $\mathrm{df}/N_{\mathrm{doc}} \ge 0.6$, $\mathrm{AV} \ge 4$, $\mathrm{lexTotal} \ge 4$ on both flanks, $H_{\min} \ge 1.8\text{ bits}$, and neighbor concentration $\le 0.45$ (single-word in spaced corpora, or $\mathrm{unitLen} \le 2$ with $\mathrm{unitLen}=1 \lor \mathrm{PMI}_{\min} < \tau_{\mathrm{PMI}}$ in unspaced corpora) are discovered unsupervised as statistical function words (\NULLTOK{}), while multi-unit spans with $\mathrm{AV} \ge 2$ and $\mathrm{PMI}_{\min} \ge \tau_{\mathrm{PMI}} = 0.35$ enter the cohesive chunk set. Complete-linkage ADIOS clustering~\citep{solan2005adios} groups tokens sharing directional neighbor signatures without single-linkage chaining collapse.

At segmentation time, both spaced texts (over word spans $0\dots m$ weighted by non-space codepoint length $L$) and unspaced streams (over codepoints $0\dots n$) run a \textbf{global Viterbi DP} sharing the same linear slope $3.8 L$ and boundary penalty $-1.8$:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:viterbi_scores}
  \sigma(s) =
  \begin{cases}
    3.8\,L - 1.8 + 0.8\,e_s - \delta_{\mathrm{hyp}}, & s \in \text{Lexicon (grounded or hypothesis)},\\
    3.8\,L - 4.45 + b_{\mathrm{coh}}(s), & s \in \text{Multi-Unit Cohesive Chunks},\\
    2.1\,L - 0.5, & s \in \text{Single-Word Spaced Cohesive Chunk},\\
    -0.8\,L \;\;(\text{unspaced}) \;/\; -0.8 \;\;(\text{spaced}), & s \in \NULLTOK{},\\
    -2.4\,L, & \text{Residual ungrounded unit},
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $\delta_{\mathrm{hyp}} = 0$ for grounded entries (and $0.6$ for single/unspaced or $2.2$ for multi-word spaced hypothesis entries), and the cohesion bonus $b_{\mathrm{coh}}(s) \in [-0.25, 0.70]$ is defined by
\begin{align*}
  b_{\mathrm{coh}}(s) =
  \begin{cases}
    \min\!\big(0.35,\, 0.15\log_2\max(2,\mathrm{AV})\big) \\
    \quad + \min\!\big(0.35,\, 0.15\max(0,\mathrm{PMI}_{\min})\big) \\
    \quad - \min\!\big(0.25,\, 0.02\,\mathrm{codeBits}\big), & \text{(unspaced char DP)},\\
    \mathrm{clip}_{[-0.25, 0.70]}(0.25\,\mathrm{PMI}_{\min}), & \text{(spaced multi-word DP)}.
  \end{cases}
\end{align*}
Running global word-level Viterbi DP over spaced spans eliminates left-to-right greedy overlap theft and prevents character-splitting English stems (e.g., \texttt{"cannot"}), while preserving the exact algebraic chunk-protection margins of unspaced character DP.

\paragraph{R2: Fisher-Exact Explaining-Away Synonyms, Privacy-Scoped PPR, and Beam-4 Parsing.}
Across grounded interactive episodes, Atri discovers open-vocabulary synonyms and polysemous mappings via \emph{episode-local explaining-away}~\citep{yu2007rapid}: already-known surface forms explain their co-occurring concepts within each episode, and residual \texttt{(token, concept)} pairs are admitted only if they exhibit zero counter-examples ($b = 0$), $\ge 2$ distinct positive evidence sequences ($a \ge 2$), $\ge 2$ negative control episodes ($c + d \ge 2$), Laplace-smoothed contrast $\Delta \ge 0.25$, and one-tailed exact hypergeometric Fisher $p \le 0.05$. Distributional Harnad transfer candidates~\citep{harnad1990symbol} are strictly tagged non-assertive (\texttt{transfer\_candidate}) until verified interactively.

To parse declarative statements and nested clauses (depths $0, 1, 2$) across arbitrary word orders (SVO, SOV, VSO), a Beam-4 chart parser matches learned multi-layer schemas~\citep{doumen2024modelling} with argument conservation and disambiguates polysemous tokens via two-hop PPR strictly filtered by $\mathrm{privacyScopeToChannel}(\kappa)$ (fail-closed to \texttt{public\_search}), completely preventing private beliefs from leaking through disambiguation side channels. Verified R2 extractions enter the hypergraph strictly as \texttt{read\_text} ($c \le 0.40$).

\paragraph{R3 \& Optional Severable R4 Scaffold.}
Level R3 deterministically ingests structured Markdown tables and JSON triples with zero LLM calls. Level R4 is an optional early-life cloud-LLM extractor available \emph{only} prior to $\Tcut$: every R4 candidate must pass exact UTF-16 source-quote verification, enters strictly as low-authority \texttt{cloud\_extract} ($c \le 0.35$, never overriding direct experience), and is physically blocked ($0$ calls, hard reject) for all $t \ge \Tcut$.

\paragraph{ISU/QUD Inquiry, Dingemanse Repair, Rosie Transfer, and Levelt--Nozari Self-Monitoring.}
Epistemic gaps open typed entries on a per-partner, privacy-isolated ISU/QUD stack~\citep{larsson2000trindi} routed via $\GRB(\pi)$ among local checking, privacy-gated external search (using two-layer defense-in-depth: lattice filtering plus NFKD/NFKC obfuscation-resistant restricted-token scrubbing with zero-echo audit logs), or low-intrusion proactive clarification. Ambiguous or revised partner turns trigger Schlangen incremental unit superseding~\citep{schlangen2011incremental}, three-level Dingemanse targeted repair~\citep{dingemanse2015repair}, and cross-day Rosie action rules~\citep{mohan2012rosie} conditioned on partner preferences. During expression, an 8-slot Centering Theory + ACT-R activation buffer~\citep{grosz1995centering,anderson2004actr} resolves anaphora without hard-coded pronoun tables, recursive \texttt{collectClausePrivacy} gates nested clauses against egress channels, and a Levelt--Nozari loopback monitor~\citep{levelt1983monitoring,nozari2011error} re-segments and re-parses every candidate utterance (performing best-first synonym 1-swap/2-swap repair and cross-clause boundary adhesion checks), abstaining cleanly whenever round-trip semantic equivalence fails~\citep{geifman2017selective}.

\begin{theorem}[Exact Bijective Script-Invariance and Viterbi Chunk-Protection Margins]
\label{thm:script}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[(a)] Let $\phi: \Sigma_1 \to \Sigma_2$ be any bijection between non-delimiter alphabets that commutes with NFKC normalization and \texttt{.toLowerCase()} and preserves delimiter classification (\texttt{DELIM\_CHAR\_RE}) and spacing typology. On any corpus and interactive episode sequence $(\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E})$ whose Viterbi, ADIOS similarity/edge-weight, Fisher, and Beam-4 candidate scores are tie-free, Atri's R1 model statistics ($\mathrm{AV},\allowbreak\, \mathrm{PMI}_{\min},\allowbreak\, H_{\min},\allowbreak\, \NULLTOK{},\allowbreak\, \text{cohesiveChunks}$), complete-linkage ADIOS clusters, R2 Fisher-exact lexicon, Beam-4 parses, and 8-dimension post-severing autonomy metrics are exact isomorphisms under $\phi$ ($\max_k |\Delta_k| = 0$).
  \item[(b)] Under the unified Viterbi scoring weights~\eqref{eq:viterbi_scores}, for every multi-unit span length $L \ge 2$ and every grounded entrenchment $e_i \in [0, 1]$:
  \begin{itemize}

    \item \emph{Cohesive Chunk Protection:} an ungrounded multi-unit cohesive chunk of length $L$ strictly beats tearing off an $(L - L_{\mathrm{null}})$ grounded prefix plus a \NULLTOK{} token of length $L_{\mathrm{null}} \ge 1$ (or residual token) by at least $0.90$;

  \end{itemize}

\end{theorem}

\section{Limitations, Ethical Considerations, and Broader Impact}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
We highlight five substantive limitations to guide future work:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Clean-Room Benchmarks vs.\ Noisy Open-Web Corpora.} While Table~\ref{tab:q307_multilang_external} validates R1 segmentation, ADIOS clustering, and 2,558-node flood survival across four natural-language genres, full end-to-end post-severing autonomy ($N=20$) was measured in controlled deterministic clean-room environments with structured feedback signals. Scaling zero-LLM R2 parsing to unconstrained, noisy web prose containing sarcasm, ellipsis, and discourse pragmatics beyond depth-2 clausal nesting remains an open research problem.
  \item \textbf{Synthetic 40-Task Diagnostic Proxy \& ACL-Filtered SQL Baselines.} Table~\ref{tab:q307_multilang_external} Part~B evaluates a clean-room SQLite diagnostic proxy rather than executing full external benchmarks such as LongMemEval~\citep{wu2025longmemeval}, Zep/Graphiti~\citep{rasmussen2025zep}, or HippoRAG~\citep{gutierrez2024hipporag} (which require online LLMs unavailable after $\Tcut$). Moreover, an engineered \emph{Metadata/ACL + Temporal-Filtered SQL RAG} baseline can enforce row-level single-hop access control and timestamp filtering; Atri's unique structural advantage over metadata-filtered SQL lies specifically in transitive multi-hop join-semilattice propagation ($\kappa_1 \sqcup \kappa_2 \to \top$), authority-tier evidence stratification, and exact downstream Nuxoll provenance-cascade invalidation across derived inferences.
  \item \textbf{Prompted 4C4G SLM Upper Bound \& Larger Model Scaling.} Our physical 4C4G CPU-only comparison (Table~\ref{tab:slm_4c4g}) evaluated 0.5B and 1.5B Q4 models because 3B--7B quantized models saturate or exceed our 3.8\,GiB zero-swap RAM ceiling and multi-second CPU latency budget (1.5B already required 1.2\,GB RAM and 5.17\,s P95 latency). Moreover, our 40-task SLM benchmark provided generous single-entity prompts with explicit rule text rather than testing unassisted multi-document RAG + SLM pipelines; larger GPU-hosted LLMs could achieve higher prompt accuracy while still lacking algebraic non-bypassable privacy guarantees and offline zero-GPU 4C4G viability.
  \item \textbf{Symbolic Root Anchors vs.\ Continuous Sensorimotor Grounding.} Atri's 40 innate root anchors abstract discrete interactive percepts, tool outcomes, and epistemic states. Bridging these anchors to continuous high-dimensional vision or robotic actuation requires coupling the metabolic hypergraph to low-level perceptual encoders while preserving exact provenance stamps.
  \item \textbf{Expressive Fluency vs.\ Epistemic Calibration.} Because Atri's zero-LLM expression module prioritizes round-trip Levelt--Nozari semantic verifiability and 0\% hallucination over stylistic variety, its surface utterances are concise and compositional rather than conversationally ornate. Hybrid deployments may optionally attach a sandboxed local SLM purely as a surface paraphrase renderer \emph{strictly downstream} of Atri's Nissenbaum egress gate and loopback verifier.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Ethical Considerations and Broader Impact.}
By design, Atri addresses two acute safety risks of deployed AI agents: cross-context privacy breaches and sycophantic manipulation. Formalizing contextual integrity as a non-bypassable join-semilattice with zero-echo audit logs protects user confidentiality even when internal concepts aggregate cross-domain structure. Conversely, persistent endogenous preference attractors ($\mu^*, \Sigma^*$) and anti-sycophancy mechanisms require transparent inspection and user-controlled episodic revocation (\texttt{learning.revoke}) so that human operators retain ultimate oversight and corrective authority over long-lived autonomous agents. All experiments in this paper use exclusively synthetic clean-room worlds and public-style technical prose with zero personal user data or credentials.
